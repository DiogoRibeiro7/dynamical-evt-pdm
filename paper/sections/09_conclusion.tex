\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Dynamical-EVT-inspired clustering diagnostics can reveal dependence structure, threshold sensitivity, and alarm-conversion failure modes, but on the evaluated public industrial datasets they did not reliably translate into operational early warning. The supported contribution is narrow: useful deployment requires identifiable failure-proximal recurrence, sufficient independent events or entities, stable regime definitions, leakage-free tuning, and episode-level alarm policies. The current evidence supports those requirements as boundary conditions, not a claim of broad predictive superiority.
