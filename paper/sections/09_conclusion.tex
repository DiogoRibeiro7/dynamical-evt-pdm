\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents a reproducible, regime-conditioned dynamical-EVT diagnostic workflow for predictive maintenance and condition monitoring. The supported contribution is broader than the initial cache-only version but still bounded: generated artifacts connect causal machine-state observables, clustered extremes, alarm episodes, sensitivity analyses, calibration diagnostics, claim constraints, and a five-dataset real-data evidence matrix. The expanded matrix covers compressor telemetry, fleet repair histories, hydraulic component-condition cycles, and semiconductor process/yield examples, with terminal verification and conservative diagnostic rows for each. The evidence does not support causal mechanical diagnosis, population-level superiority, or validity beyond the evaluated settings. Those claims require additional datasets, prospective deployment evidence, and stronger predeclared comparative baselines under the same frozen protocol.
