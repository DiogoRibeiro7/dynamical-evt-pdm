\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presents a reproducible, regime-conditioned dynamical-EVT diagnostic workflow for predictive maintenance. The supported contribution is narrow: generated artifacts can connect causal machine-state observables, clustered extremes, alarm episodes, sensitivity analyses, calibration diagnostics, and claim constraints. The evidence does not support broader industrial performance claims, causal mechanical diagnosis, or population-level validity beyond the evaluated setting. Those claims require additional real-data experiment completion under the same frozen protocol.
