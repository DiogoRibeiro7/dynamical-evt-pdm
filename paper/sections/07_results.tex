\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The generated results support a diagnostic and reproducibility claim, not a broad industrial performance claim. Synthetic figures and tables are produced by version-controlled code under experiment identifier \DynEvtExperimentId{} and dataset checksum \DynEvtDatasetChecksum{}. Real-data tables are produced by the registered matrix after local public-data acquisition, preparation, manifest verification, and conservative dataset-specific diagnostics.

\subsection{Five-Dataset Real-Data Matrix}

The real-data matrix verifies five public datasets and records four distinct sampling structures: ordered compressor telemetry, fleet-level irregular vehicle histories, hydraulic test-rig cycles with multi-rate sensor traces, and semiconductor process measurements per production entity. Table~\ref{tab:evidence-scope} shows that the independence units also differ: failure episode or operating day, vehicle, load cycle, and wafer or production example. This is the intended broadening of the study base. It does not make the datasets exchangeable; it makes their non-equivalence auditable.

Table~\ref{tab:industrial-results} reports the conservative diagnostic row produced for each registered real dataset. MetroPT and MetroPT2 are evaluated as event-level early-warning tasks on temporal test splits. SCANIA is evaluated as vehicle-level repair risk on the published test split. Hydraulic Systems is evaluated as cycle-level hydraulic component degradation, and SECOM as wafer-level semiconductor yield-failure detection. These rows are useful precisely because they preserve weak results. The compressor rows recover the single labelled test event with very low precision, while Hydraulic Systems, SCANIA, and SECOM have \(F_1=0\) under the registered high-quantile diagnostic. The result is not a performance victory. It is evidence that the pipeline now runs across materially different public industrial-monitoring settings and records when a simple extreme-score diagnostic is insufficient.

The real-data evidence therefore changes the manuscript claim in a specific way. The study is broader than a single synthetic cache or one compressor case study: it now covers five fetched and prepared public datasets with manifest checks, schema summaries, and terminal diagnostic rows. At the same time, CLM-007 remains active because the diagnostic table contains heterogeneous estimands and weak rows, not a controlled demonstration of population-level superiority.
