\section{Robustness and Negative Results}
\label{supp:robustness}

The robustness policy is to preserve unfavorable outcomes. Threshold sensitivity, run-length sensitivity, regime-definition changes, dangerous-region definitions, missingness perturbations, and noise perturbations are all represented as registered families in the generated ablation table. Some cells are summaries of planned or non-estimable effects rather than completed industrial contrasts, so the ablation table is treated as a coverage record rather than a positive performance result.

Negative and null findings are part of the evidence. The manuscript does not turn diagnostic evidence into broad industrial performance claims, and it does not use empirical reliability diagnostics as calibrated probability evidence. These constraints are enforced by the paper-check command and by generated audit artifacts.
