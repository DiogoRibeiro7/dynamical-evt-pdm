\section{Robustness and Negative Results}
\label{supp:robustness}

The robustness policy is to preserve unfavorable outcomes. Threshold sensitivity, run-length sensitivity, regime-definition changes, dangerous-region definitions, missingness perturbations, and noise perturbations are all represented as registered families in the generated ablation table. Some cells are summaries of planned or non-estimable effects rather than completed industrial contrasts; this is why CLM-006 is a coverage claim rather than a positive performance claim.

Negative and null findings are part of the evidence. CLM-007 blocks the manuscript from claiming population-level industrial superiority. CLM-005 blocks strong calibration language when only an empirical reliability diagnostic is available. These constraints are enforced by the paper-check command and by the claim ledger generated from the asset builder.
