# Literature map

## Dynamical EVT foundations

### Hitting Time Statistics and Extreme Value Theory

Ana Cristina Moreira Freitas, Jorge Milhazes Freitas and Mike Todd.

- Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.2887>
- Role: formal connection between entry into shrinking sets and extreme-value laws.

### Extremal Index, Hitting Time Statistics and Periodicity

Ana Cristina Moreira Freitas, Jorge Milhazes Freitas and Mike Todd.

- Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.1350>
- Role: extremal-index existence, periodic phenomena and clustered extremes.

### Extreme Value Statistics for Dynamical Systems with Noise

Davide Faranda, Jorge Milhazes Freitas, Valerio Lucarini, Giorgio Turchetti and Sandro Vaienti.

- Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.5582>
- Role: finite noisy observations can obscure deterministic recurrence and affect the observed extremal index.

### Multivariate Extreme Values for Dynamical Systems

Romain Aimino, Ana Cristina Moreira Freitas, Jorge Milhazes Freitas and Mike Todd.

- Preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14807>
- Role: dynamically adapted cross-sectional dependence from combined spatial and temporal structure.

## Industrial datasets

### MetroPT

Bruno Veloso, Rita P. Ribeiro, João Gama and Pedro Mota Pereira.

- Article: <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-022-01877-3>
- Data: <https://zenodo.org/records/6854240>
- Role: principal APU telemetry case study with reported failure intervals and a two-hour operational warning target.

### MetroPT2

- Data: <https://zenodo.org/records/7766691>
- Role: temporal replication with two documented failure periods.

### SCANIA Component X

Zahra Kharazian, Tony Lindgren and Oskar Andersson Reyna.

- Article: <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04802-6>
- Role: external fleet-level validation with operational histories, repairs and vehicle specifications.
